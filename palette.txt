Winter
SMELLS LIKE: winter, minty cool, outdoors
USE IN SAFE SPACE: snow-muffled quiet, cold that feels sheltered rather than harsh; a window with snow outside and warmth behind it; minty "clean slate" calm when the client wants the air itself to feel fresh

Barnyard
SMELLS LIKE: a barnyard
USE IN SAFE SPACE: when the client's safe place IS a ranch, farm, or animal sanctuary, or they name barn animals as what makes it feel right — for those clients it can be the most home-feeling scent in the palette. Otherwise avoid; manure imagery pulls people out of a safe space

Sweet
SMELLS LIKE: a chocolate chip cookie.
USE IN SAFE SPACE: comfort and being cared for; baking with someone who loved you, childhood kitchens, a reward the client earned. Good gentle default when a moment should not go silent but has no stronger hook

Floral
SMELLS LIKE: a sweet flower
USE IN SAFE SPACE: gardens in bloom, meadows, open windows over flowers, lavender unh winding, spring mornings; any place the client named blossoms or a garden

Beach
SMELLS LIKE: notes of saltwater with a strong, sweet sunscreen smell
USE IN SAFE SPACE: tropical warmth, a lazy seaside afternoon, sunscreen-and-hot-sand ease; clean warm indoor air as a gentle neutral when nothing else fits

Kindred
SMELLS LIKE: baby powder and lavender
USE IN SAFE SPACE: being held, family and close friends, babies and caregiving, the powdery-lavender comfort of someone who loves you; name it whenever a person is what makes the place safe

Petrichor
SMELLS LIKE: rain
USE IN SAFE SPACE: first rain after heat, storms passing, wet stone and streets after rain; the smell of relief itself. Strong pick when the client's calm feels like weather changing

Marine
SMELLS LIKE: the ocean
USE IN SAFE SPACE: waves keeping time with breathing, open coast air, tide pools, being small beside something vast in a safe way

Evergreen
SMELLS LIKE: a pinecone
USE IN SAFE SPACE: deep quiet woods, pine and fir, moss underfoot, a cabin smell of conifers, forests that feel protective because they are dense

Terra Silva
SMELLS LIKE: a mossy riverbed
USE IN SAFE SPACE: creek banks, dark rich earth, bare feet in a garden, the grounded slow calm of soil and moving fresh water

Citrus
SMELLS LIKE: citrus fruit
USE IN SAFE SPACE: bright kitchens, morning light, peeling something fresh; uplift without weight. Use when the client's safe feeling is happy-energy rather than hush

Desert
SMELLS LIKE: the desert, cactus kind of heavy
USE IN SAFE SPACE: wide-open quiet, starlit dry heat, canyon stillness, for clients who feel safest in big empty arid places

Savory Spice
SMELLS LIKE: a bunch of spices, ginger forward
USE IN SAFE SPACE: home cooking, cinnamon and ginger in a loved one's kitchen, holiday food memories, any place the client named a meal or a person's cooking

Timber
SMELLS LIKE: wood, slightly sweet
USE IN SAFE SPACE: wood cabins, cedar closets, old solid furniture, a house built of timber; the smell of shelter itself

Smoky
SMELLS LIKE: smoke
USE IN SAFE SPACE: campfire in measured doses only — fireplace crackle, embers, a fire pit with trusted people. Never industrial or wildfire smoke imagery

Machina
SMELLS LIKE: mechinal parts, an oily, pungent smell
USE IN SAFE SPACE: almost never; reserve for clients whose safe place genuinely involves a workshop, garage, or engine work they love. Fouler smell, don't overuse
